A patchable modular synth for the M-VAVE FM-1

PATCHWERK is a modular synthesizer for the M-VAVE FM-1 with over 80 modules. Build and play patches directly on the FM-1, or use the browser emulator, which runs the same firmware and includes a visual patch bay. Connect your FM-1 over USB to control it from the browser, with changes synced both ways.

How it works

PATCHWERK is a modular: you build a sound by placing modules (oscillators, filters, effects, sequencers, modulators) and joining them with cables. Everything is a voltage, so any output can feed any input: a sequencer can play a filter, an envelope can steer a delay. HOME is always the patch screen, and you can build and edit patches right on the FM-1. Press a page's button again to turn to its next page.

  1. Add. Press FX to open the library, turn SELECT to browse and press SEL to add. With a cable selected the module is wired in between, like this VCF between VCO and VCA; ENV adds it unpatched.
  2. Patch. HOME is the patch screen. Turn SELECT to a jack, press SEL to grab its cable, move to another jack and press SEL again. REC unplugs; GLO + OCT− undoes anything. Hold HOME for a moment to see CPU and battery.
  3. Shape. Knobs 1–4 set the module's parameters; ALGORITHM, or a tap of HOME, changes page, and GLO gives fine moves. EDIT shows the module's own screen, here the VCF's filter curve; EDIT again shows its connections.
  4. Sequence. SEQ gives 16 steps on the lower keys; press it again for the next sequencer. REC records steps or live playing.
  5. Perform. PERFORM gathers eight favorite knobs from anywhere in the patch (hold ENV and turn a knob to add it). Press ENV again for MUTES, to bring parts in and out, then MUTATE, to discover new variations.
  6. Song. Sixteen snapshots remember every knob, step and bypass: the keys recall them in PERFORM, SEL + key recalls on the patch screen, and GLO + key saves into one. SONG (LFO) arranges snapshots as clips and automates knobs over time.
  7. Matrix. ARP lists every module and cable with its amount, to change or unplug from one place. GLO + FX turns a chain into a group, one box you can fold and open; ready-made groups are in the patch list, after the factory patches.
  8. Save and load. Turn PRESETS for the patch list and SEL to load. SAVE opens the save page: pick a slot (U1–U8), name it, SEL stores. Set KIND to GROUP and the list inserts it into the open patch as one box. SAVE again pages through Settings and Help, which lists every shortcut.

GLO plus another control does copy, bypass, groups and tempo. The on-unit help (PRESETS, then ALGORITHM) and the cheatsheet list them all.

Web patch bay

The same modules and firmware as the FM-1, drawn as boxes and cables on a bigger canvas. Drag between jacks to connect, drag a module to move it, click a cable to remove it.

  • Linked: connect over USB (Link FM-1, top of the page) and the bay and the unit stay in sync both ways.
  • Unlinked: it plays through your computer, so you can build before you own one.

Click a module to add it, or drag it onto the bay. Drag between jacks to patch. Grab a plugged input to move or pull its cable; Cmd/Ctrl+drag from an output to pick up the cable already on it. Click a cable to remove it. Linked to an FM-1, every change here happens on the FM-1 too, and its changes show here.

Sample banks

Four banks of eight sounds, A through D, shared by SAMPLER, GRANULAR and DRUM RACK.

Click a slot to load a sound, or drag and drop up to eight audio files onto a bank. Trim removes silence, and Compress reduces file size. Presets load the included banks: A is an 808 kit, B a 909 kit, C FM drums and D a mix of odd sounds. Drum banks run kick, snare, clap, closed hat, open hat, tom, cowbell, crash, so pads 1–8 of a DRUM RACK line up.

When connected to an FM-1, Send saves samples to its flash memory. You can also record directly on the FM-1 using AUDIO IN and SAMPLER.

CZ banks

The CZ synth includes 81 built-in presets and four banks for your own sounds.

Click a slot or drop Casio CZ SysEx files (.syx) to load up to 128 presets per bank. Supports CZ-101, CZ-1000, CZ-3000, CZ-5000 and CZ-1 files.

When connected to an FM-1, imported banks are automatically saved to its flash memory.

FM-DX banks

FM-DX includes 32 built-in presets and four banks for your own sounds.

Click a slot or drop Yamaha SysEx files (.syx) to load presets from the TX81Z, DX21, DX27 or DX100. Supports full banks and individual voices.

When connected to an FM-1, imported banks are automatically saved to its flash memory.

    Install on an FM-1

    Install PATCHWERK from this page over USB. It takes about two minutes and your FM-1 keeps its own firmware recoverable.

    Download M-VAVE's firmware from their site (FM-1 V15: FM-1.fwsc).

    If something goes wrong
    • USB cable disconnected, or the screen stays black. The FM-1 is probably still in update mode. Leave it connected, reopen this page, select the firmware file and click Install to resume. Don't turn it off.
    • "FM-1 not found" with a black screen. Check whether the FM-1 appears as a USB drive named WL82 UBOOT1.00. If it does, you can restore the original firmware using MvaveFM1Unbricker (Windows/Linux) or FM-1 Switch (Mac/Windows).
    • FM-1 doesn't appear over USB. Recovery may still be possible using a small RP2040 board connected to the FM-1's USB lines. See the Transporter guide or FM1 Unbricking for Dummies. The recovery process backs up the flash before making changes and asks for confirmation.
    • Bootloader protection. PATCHWERK's installer leaves the first 16 KB of flash untouched, preserving the FM-1's boot code. The recovery tools linked above are independent community projects. Read their instructions before using them.

    Build the firmware yourself (developers)
  
    PATCHWERK is an app on fm1-base, the community platform for FM-1 firmware (on felucca-bsp, from Felucca). It builds on a Mac or Linux; on a Mac, Docker runs JieLi's compiler for the chip. You install it over USB, from a terminal or from Chrome.
