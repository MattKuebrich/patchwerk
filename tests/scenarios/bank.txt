# The sample bank in the flash (core/pw_bank.c): a bank written to the data area as the web page would leave
# it (tests/make_bank.py), a SAMPLER set to B1 plays it.
# (fm1-base's host "data" command puts a file 0x4000 below the offset it is given: it indexes its simulated flash
# from PLAT_DATA_LO, which starts at PLAT_XDATA0_LO. So 0xAB000 here is 0xA7000 in the flash.)
data tests/out/bank_dir.bin 0xAB000
data tests/out/bank_data.bin 0xAC000
reboot
wait 2500
expect bank 2
# new patch, a SAMPLER (the fifth source: SEL wires its OUT into OUT L under the cursor), its BANK (K1) to A1 and SAMPLE (K2) to 1
turn PRESET 1
wait 100
turn SELECT -2
tap SEL
wait 300
tap FX
wait 100
wait 100
turn SELECT 12 150
wait 100
shot add_sampler.png
tap SEL
wait 200
expect type 24
turn K1 1
wait 100
turn K2 1
wait 200
shot sampler_b1.png
expect param0 1000
# its trigger: KEYS TRIG into the sampler's GATE (the add list goes round its ends: the end of the list moves when modules are added shown)
tap FX
wait 100
turn SELECT 64 150
wait 100
tap SEL
wait 200
expect type 26
# KEYS: outputs PITCH GATE VEL TRIG MOD; the cursor on PITCH: 3 on is TRIG; SEL picks it up
turn SELECT 3 250
tap SEL
wait 100
