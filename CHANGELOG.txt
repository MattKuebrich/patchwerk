v1.0.0-rc36
  CZ (checked against MAME's uPD933): NOISE modulation now picks its random pitch (base or 32 semitones up) every 8 samples of the chip's 40 kHz clock, 5 kHz, instead of every sample, so it is a rougher, lower noise like the chip's. The DCW is capped as the pitch rises (1024 minus four times the phase step per chip sample), as the chip does, so high notes of narrow-pulse and resonance tones stay clean.
